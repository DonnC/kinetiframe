# Current character animation guide

Use the approved four-view Tavi, Mira and Kuda source kits. Keep each authored view consistent;
do not fake front/back views by relabelling profile artwork. Current clothing, ears, collars,
shoes and hands follow the approved kits, not the superseded simplified-host drawing budget.

Record purposeful gestures with preparation, movement and settle. Use held poses for pauses;
avoid constant bobbing. Change hand artwork through actor-owned alternatives, and use shared
expressions, eyes and mouths. Dialogue can begin with scratch narration; choose mouth changes
at audible beats and keep the mouth at rest during silence.

Continuous drawing and cutout artwork are both supported workflows. Inspect neck, shoulders,
wrists, waist and ankles in neutral and bent poses. Check overlap order, pivots and skin binding
before blaming source art or disguising gaps with extra stamps. Every source repair must be
verified in native render proofs and device playback.

Layer brief hand-drawn accents over performances rather than flattening the editable scene.
Camera ranges should support the lesson or gesture, not hide a broken actor assembly.
