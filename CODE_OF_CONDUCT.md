# Code of conduct

KinetiFrame is made by animators, artists and developers from different countries, backgrounds
and levels of experience. We want everyone who takes part to feel welcome and respected.

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other project space, and when
you represent the project elsewhere.

## In short

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to animation or to programming.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work, never about the person.
- Credit other people's work, and accept corrections gracefully.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention, publishing someone's private
information and sustained disruption are not acceptable, whatever the setting.

## Reporting

If you experience or see behavior that breaks this code, email
[donnclab@gmail.com](mailto:donnclab@gmail.com). Reports are read by the maintainer and kept
confidential. Please don't post the details of a report in a public issue.

If the report concerns the maintainer, say so in your email and it will be handled by another
trusted community member once the project has one, or you can contact GitHub directly through
its [reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

## Enforcement

Depending on what happened, the maintainer may give a private warning, remove content, or
temporarily or permanently ban someone from project spaces. The Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
describe these steps in more detail.
